Your Privacy Matters

Effective Date: January 28, 2026

Bitveste ("we", "us", "our") operates a platform that enables structured inventory participation, branded gift-card conversion to purchasing power, settlement and reporting services, and ancillary financial services (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights available to individuals in the United States.

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. We are committed to transparency and protecting your personal information with industry-standard safeguards.

1. Overview; Acceptance

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy and consent to our collection, processing, and use of personal information as described herein.

2. Controller and Contact

Controller: Bitvest Capital LLC

Registered Address: 13313 Bellacosa Ave, Baton Rouge, LA 70817, United States

Registered Agent: Alfonso Arechiga

Privacy Contact / Data Protection Officer: privacy@bitveste.com

Legal Notices: legal@bitveste.com

3. Scope and Applicability

This Policy applies to personal information collected through our websites, mobile applications, software, APIs, and any offline interactions related to the Service. It applies to U.S. persons and residents using the Service; where local law provides additional rights (e.g., California), we describe those rights below.

4. Definitions

  • Personal Information: Information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with an individual, as understood under applicable U.S. laws.
  • Processed / Processing: Any operation performed on Personal Information.
  • Service Providers / Processors: Third parties that process data on our behalf under contract.

5. Categories of Information Collected

We collect the following categories of personal information, as applicable:

  • A. Identity & Account Data: Name, username, date of birth, government ID (when required for verification).
  • B. Contact Data: Email, phone number, mailing address.
  • C. Credential Data: Password hashes, two-factor authentication IDs.
  • D. Financial & Payment Data: Bank account or routing numbers, ACH information, card details (tokenized by our payment partners), transaction histories, allocation and settlement records.
  • E. Verification & Compliance Data: KYC documents (passport, driver's license), tax identifiers (TIN, SSN where required), source-of-funds documentation, background and sanctions-screening results.
  • F. Transaction & Usage Data: Orders, allocations, settlement details, merchant offers, payment authorizations and reversals.
  • G. Technical & Device Data: IP addresses, device identifiers, operating system, browser type and version, cookies, crash reports, access logs.
  • H. Communications Content: Messages to/from support, call recordings (where permitted).
  • I. Risk & Suitability Data: Investor accreditation status, net worth, income, investment objectives, risk tolerance.
  • J. Publicly-Available & Third-Party Data: Data obtained from credit bureaus, identity verification providers, banks, payment processors, and public sources.

6. Sources of Information

  • Directly from you (registration, account setup, support communications, credit/loan applications).
  • Automatically from your device and usage of the Service.
  • From third parties (payment processors, banks, identity verification vendors, credit reporting agencies, public databases).

7. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal information for legitimate business purposes, performance of contract, legal compliance, and where applicable, your consent. Primary purposes include:

  • Providing, operating, and improving the Service (account onboarding, allocations, funding, settlement).
  • Processing payments, issuing credits/partial refunds, administering the Bitveste credit line.
  • KYC/AML compliance, sanctions screening, fraud prevention, and internal risk control.
  • Responding to support requests, disputes, and investigations.
  • Compliance with legal/regulatory obligations (tax reporting, subpoenas, court orders).
  • Security, abuse prevention, and platform integrity.
  • Aggregated analytics and product development (de-identified where practical).
  • Marketing and promotional communications where you have opted in; you may unsubscribe at any time.

8. Credit Decisions & Credit Line

If you apply for a Bitveste credit line or other credit product, we may perform credit checks and verify information with consumer reporting agencies. Information used in underwriting may affect credit eligibility and will be used consistent with applicable U.S. laws, including the Fair Credit Reporting Act.

Our starter credit line offering is subject to credit approval, terms, and conditions documented in the credit agreement.

9. Sharing and Disclosure

We share personal information with:

  • Service Providers: Payment processors, ACH vendors, card networks, cloud hosting, KYC/ID proofing vendors, analytics providers, customer support vendors.
  • Financial Institutions and Settlement Partners: To effect transfers, conversions of gift cards to purchasing power, and reconciliations.
  • Regulators and Law Enforcement: Where legally required (subpoena, court order), or to comply with AML/OFAC/sanctions rules.
  • Professional Advisors: Auditors, legal counsel, accountants, under confidentiality agreements.
  • Acquirers / Successors: In connection with corporate transactions (merger, sale, insolvency), subject to confidentiality obligations.

We do not sell personal information for money.

10. International Transfers

Data may be transferred to third parties or systems located outside the U.S. where necessary. When transfers occur we will apply reasonable safeguards (contracts, security measures, encryption). If you are in a jurisdiction with specific cross-border transfer rules, contact us for details.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information to fulfill the purposes described and to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Typical retention examples:

  • Transaction and AML records: retained for at least five (5) years.
  • Account records: retained while account active and for a reasonable period thereafter.
  • Specific retention may vary by type of information and legal requirement.

12. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. These include:

  • Access controls and role-based permissions.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Regular vendor security assessments.
  • Incident response procedures.

However, no security system is impenetrable; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Your Rights — U.S. and California Residents

All Users:

  • Access to account records.
  • Ability to correct or update account information.
  • Control over marketing communications.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal information collected.
  • Right to request deletion (subject to exceptions).
  • Right to request correction.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising (if applicable).
  • Right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Submit requests via privacy@bitveste.com. We will verify the requester's identity before fulfilling. We will respond to verified requests within applicable statutory timelines.

Other State Laws: Some states provide additional rights; we will comply where applicable. For data portability, we will provide data in a machine-readable format where required.

14. Cookies and Tracking

We and our partners use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies for security, functionality, analytics, and advertising. You may manage cookies through browser controls and our cookie preference center (where available).

15. Children

The Service is not aimed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have collected such information, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

16. Third-Party Sites and Services

Our Service may link to third-party sites with separate privacy practices. We are not responsible for their content or policies.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be posted with an updated effective date and, where required, notice will be provided.

18. How to Contact Us; Complaints

Submit privacy requests, complaints, or questions to:

Privacy Inquiries: privacy@bitveste.com

Legal Notices: legal@bitveste.com

You may also mail requests to the registered address listed in Section 2.
